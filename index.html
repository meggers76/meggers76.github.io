Personal Automation Tools

A private, non-commercial set of small tools that one person runs on their own computer to organise everyday life.

What this is

The tools help with routine personal tasks, such as finding documents, looking up contacts, keeping track of to-dos, or planning the week. They run locally on the owner's own devices. They are not a public service: there is nothing to sign up for, nothing is sold, and no one else's data is collected.

Connecting accounts

Some tools need to read data from online accounts, for example a Google account. They ask for permission through the provider's standard sign-in and consent screen, and they can only access what is shown and approved there. Access can be withdrawn at any time. For Google, use myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Privacy

Data from connected accounts is used only to carry out the tasks the owner asks for. It is never sold or used for advertising. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
